Where Do I See Myself in 4 Years?

In four years, I see myself working as a cybersecurity analyst in a Security Operations Center, monitoring networks and responding to threats as they happen. I want to grow from there into penetration testing, where I can help organizations discover their weak points before attackers do. To get there, I plan to earn certifications such as CompTIA Security+ and eJPT, and to keep practicing on platforms like TryHackMe and Hack The Box. I am also considering a master’s degree in cybersecurity, with a focus on cloud security. Above all, I hope to inspire more women in Jordan to build careers in the security field.
